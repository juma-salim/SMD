Delete your Send Me Deliveries account

You can delete your account and personal data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the SMD app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE, enter your password (if you have one) and confirm.

Your account is removed straight away.

Can’t log in?

Contact us at {{CONTACT}} from the email address on the account, and tell us your registered phone number. We will verify it is you and delete the account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, sign-in details and saved shops.
  • Shippers: your ID details and ID photos.
  • Senders: your delivery requests and history.

What we keep

  • A minimal M-Pesa payment record (amount, date, receipt number, phone number) for as long as accounting and legal rules require.
  • Shippers: deliveries you carried out stay on the sender’s record, with your name and phone number removed.

When deletion is not possible yet

You cannot delete your account while you have a delivery in progress, or a paid request that has not been completed. Finish or cancel it first, or contact us.
