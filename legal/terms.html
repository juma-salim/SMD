Part A: Terms of Use

1. About these terms

These terms are an agreement between you and SMD. By creating an account or using SMD you confirm that you have read and accepted them and the Privacy Policy in Part B. If you do not agree, please do not use SMD.

2. What SMD is

SMD is a technology platform. It lets senders request deliveries (with or without goods from a shop), lets verified shippers accept and carry out those deliveries, handles payment through M-Pesa, and lets shop owners list goods and prices. SMD does not own shops, and shippers are independent persons, not employees of SMD.

3. Who can use SMD

  • You must be 18 or older and able to enter a binding agreement.
  • You must give accurate information and keep it up to date. One person may not hold several accounts to get around a suspension or a rule.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password and your phone safe, and for everything done through your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else is using it.

4. Account types

  • Senders request deliveries and pay for them.
  • Shippers must first be verified with a valid identity document and a selfie. An administrator may approve or reject an application. Shippers must also meet any licensing, insurance and road-safety rules that apply to their vehicle and work.
  • Shop owners list their shop and goods. A shop appears to customers only after it has been approved.
  • Administrators and sub-administrators are SMD’s own reviewers. They may verify shippers and approve shops, but only within the areas they are responsible for. They are bound by confidentiality and by Part B.

5. Ordering a delivery

  • The delivery fee is calculated by SMD from the pickup and drop-off areas, the item weight and the speed you choose, and is shown before you submit. Goods from listed shops are priced per unit by the shop or by SMD and added to the fee.
  • You must describe the item and the drop-off accurately and give a recipient who can be reached by phone.
  • Your request stays pending until a shipper accepts it. You may cancel a pending request that has not been paid. A request that has been paid cannot be cancelled in the app; contact us for help.
  • Prohibited items. You may not send anything illegal or dangerous, including weapons, explosives, illegal drugs, stolen goods, hazardous or flammable materials, or anything that the law does not allow you to possess or transport. A shipper may refuse or stop a delivery that appears unsafe or unlawful.

6. Payments

  • Payments are made by M-Pesa. You approve each payment by entering your PIN on your own phone. We never see or keep your PIN.
  • Pay now: your request is shown to shippers once your payment is received.
  • Pay after delivery: for goods above the limit shown in the app, from listed shops, you pay through the app after you confirm that you have received your goods. If you do not complete the payment, we may suspend your account and recover the amount due.
  • If you are charged twice, charged a wrong amount, or pay for an order that no longer exists, tell us. We will check the record and refund what you are owed through M-Pesa.
  • SMD keeps a service share of each delivery fee and pays the remainder to the shipper. Shops are paid the value of the goods sold. Payouts are made by SMD after the delivery is completed.

7. Completing a delivery

A delivery is completed only when the shipper has marked it delivered and the sender (or the recipient on the sender’s behalf) has confirmed receipt. Please confirm only when you have actually received your goods, and check them before you confirm.

8. Rules for shippers

  • Handle goods with care, deliver them to the right recipient and do not open, keep, swap or damage them.
  • Use only your own approved identity. You may not let someone else use your account.
  • Be courteous, follow the traffic and safety laws, and do not collect or demand any money that is not owed through the app.
  • You are responsible for your own vehicle, fuel, licences, insurance and taxes.

9. Rules for shop owners

  • List only goods you are entitled to sell, with honest descriptions and prices. You are responsible for the quality, safety and legality of what you sell.
  • Keep your price list current. Customers are charged the price shown at the time of ordering.

10. Ratings and conduct

Senders may rate a shipper from 1 to 5 stars after a completed delivery. Ratings must be honest. Do not harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone, post false information, attempt to break or misuse the service, or use it for fraud.

11. Suspension and ending your account

We may warn, suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, puts others at risk, or is used for fraud. You may delete your account at any time in the app (Settings), subject to finishing any delivery or payment in progress and to any earnings still waiting to be paid to you.

12. Our responsibility

We work to keep SMD available and accurate, but we cannot promise it will always be uninterrupted or error free. SMD connects senders, shippers and shops; goods are carried by independent shippers and sold by independent shops. To the fullest extent the law allows, SMD is not responsible for loss or damage caused by a shipper, shop or other user, or for delays outside our control, and our total responsibility to you for any claim is limited to the amount you paid us for the delivery concerned. Nothing in these terms removes any right you have under Kenyan consumer-protection law, or limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

13. Changes

We may update these terms and the Privacy Policy. When we make an important change we will show the new version and ask you to accept it before you continue. The version number above tells you which one applies.

14. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya, and the courts of Kenya have jurisdiction. Please contact us first so that we can try to resolve any problem together.

Part B: Privacy Policy

We handle personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya and its regulations. This part explains what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what choices you have. For the purposes of that law, SMD is the data controller.

1. What we collect

Everyone

  • Account details: name, phone number, email address, and a password (stored only as a salted one-way hash, never in readable form). If you sign in with Google, Google gives us your verified email and name.
  • Technical data: a secure login cookie, push-notification settings if you turn them on, and limited server logs (time, request address, errors) used for security and for fixing faults.

Senders

  • Delivery details: pickup and drop-off areas, shop, items ordered, landmarks, the recipient’s name and phone number, and, only if you choose to share it, a map pin.
  • Payment records: the M-Pesa number you pay from, amount, receipt number, date and status. Card details are not used. Your M-Pesa PIN is entered only on your phone and handled by Safaricom, never by us.
  • Ratings you give to shippers.

Shippers

  • Vehicle type and base area.
  • For identity verification only: ID type and number, date of birth, home address, and photos of your ID (front and back) and a selfie holding your ID.
  • Your job history, ratings you receive, and earnings and payout records.

Shop owners

  • Your shop name and area, your goods and prices, and records of sales and payouts.

Location

We ask for your device location only when you tap “Share my current location” to add a map pin. We do not track you in the background.

2. Why we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, confirm your email, and sign you in.
  • To match senders with shippers and complete deliveries.
  • To take payments through M-Pesa, keep records, pay shippers and shops, and refund mistakes.
  • To verify shippers’ identity so that senders can trust the person collecting their goods.
  • To send service messages (verification codes, approval decisions, job alerts you have switched on).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, keep the service safe, resolve disputes, and meet legal and accounting duties.

We use your data only for these purposes. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Identity documents (shippers)

This applies to everything you give us to verify your identity.

  • Purpose limitation. We collect your ID details and photos only to confirm who you are and that you are an adult. We do not use them for marketing, profiling or any other purpose.
  • Who can see them. Only the SMD administrator, and the sub-administrator responsible for your base area, can view them, and only while reviewing your application or handling a dispute or security concern. They are stored privately and are never placed in a public folder.
  • No sharing without consent. We do not share your identity details or photos with senders, shops, other shippers or any other third party without your consent. The only exception is where we are legally required to disclose them, for example by a court order or to a lawful request from the police or another authority. Other users see only your name, vehicle, phone number (after you accept their job), rating and number of completed deliveries.
  • Deletion. When you delete your account, your ID details and photos are deleted with it. You can also ask us to delete them if your application is rejected.

4. Who else sees your data

  • Other users, only as needed for a delivery. The job board shows shippers the route, items and fee but not the sender’s or recipient’s identity or the exact drop-off. After a shipper accepts a job they see the sender’s and recipient’s names and phone numbers and the drop-off. The sender sees the shipper’s name, phone number and vehicle. Customers browsing “near you” see a shipper’s short name, vehicle, rating and delivery count only. Shop owners do not receive customers’ personal details through the app.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract and only on our instructions: our hosting provider (Render), our database provider (Turso), our email provider (Brevo), Safaricom (M-Pesa payments), Google (only if you use Google sign-in), and the push-notification service of your browser or phone if you turn alerts on.
  • Authorities, where the law requires.

Some of these providers may store or process data outside Kenya. Where that happens we take steps to make sure your data stays protected as the law requires.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, delivery and rating data: while your account exists.
  • Identity details and photos: while your shipper account exists, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Payment, payout and refund records: kept for as long as the law requires for accounting, tax and dispute purposes, even after an account is deleted.
  • Server logs: kept for a short period, then removed.

6. How we protect it

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords only as salted hashes, store identity photos privately, limit staff access by role and area, limit repeated login attempts, and verify email addresses. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy);
  • access the data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or out of date (you can edit your name and phone in Settings);
  • delete your data (Settings, “Delete my account”, or the deletion page);
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain uses;
  • receive a copy of your data in a common format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before, and some services (for example shipper work) cannot continue without the data they need.

To use these rights, email us at the address below. We will answer within the time the law allows. If you are unhappy with our answer you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya).

8. Cookies and similar technology

SMD uses one essential cookie to keep you logged in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. If you use Google sign-in, Google’s own scripts load on the sign-in screen and are covered by Google’s policy.

9. Children

SMD is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

If we change how we use your data we will update this page, change the version number and ask for your acceptance again where the law or the size of the change requires it.
