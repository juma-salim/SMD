# Google Play listing: Send Me Deliveries (updated 16 Nov 2026)

**App name (max 30):** Send Me Deliveries
**Short description (max 80):** Get goods and parcels delivered from local shops. Pay with M-Pesa.
**Full description (max 4000):**
Send Me Deliveries (SMD) makes it easy to get what you need from shops along the coast, from Mariakani and Mtwapa through Mombasa and Likoni to Tiwi, Ukunda and Diani, delivered to your door. Si lazima uende dukani.

For senders
• Choose a shop, pick your goods and a drop-off point, and send the request in seconds
• See the delivery fee before you order
• Pay with M-Pesa: a PIN prompt comes straight to your phone
• For bigger orders from our listed shops, pay after your goods arrive
• See recommended shops and available shippers with their star ratings
• Track every request, confirm when you have received your goods, and rate your shipper
• Ask the built-in assistant, in English or Kiswahili, about fees, orders and how SMD works

For shippers (riders and drivers)
• Verify your identity once, then see jobs near you
• Accept jobs, see pickup and drop-off details, and mark deliveries complete
• Earn on every delivery, tracked in the app, with payouts from SMD after completion

For shop owners
• List your goods and prices so customers can order from you

Simple and secure: passwords are stored safely, ID photos are seen only by SMD reviewers and used only to verify shippers, and payments are handled by M-Pesa. See our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy in the app.
